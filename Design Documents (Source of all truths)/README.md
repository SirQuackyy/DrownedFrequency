# The Drowned Frequency

A production blueprint for a 4–6 hour first-person acoustic horror game built in Unreal Engine 5.

## Start here

1. Read `01_MASTER_GDD.md` for the complete game.
2. Read `02_VERTICAL_SLICE.md` and build only that first.
3. Follow `03_TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md` for the Unreal architecture.
4. Use `04_PRODUCTION_ROADMAP.md` as the milestone and backlog plan.

## One-sentence pitch

Trapped in an abandoned coastal listening station during a storm, you record and project sounds to open routes and deceive a presence that learns to imitate every sound you use.

## Locked scope

- Platform: Windows PC, Steam first
- Engine: Unreal Engine 5, stable production release
- Camera: first person
- Length: 4–6 hours
- Team: one developer, optionally supported by contractors
- Structure: one compact facility, six streamed sectors
- Combat: none
- Primary threat: one entity with three manifestations
- Signature system: authored sound capture, replay, contamination, and projection
- Full-production estimate: 47 focused full-time weeks after preproduction assumptions hold; plan 12–18 months for a realistic solo schedule with interruptions

## Non-negotiable pillars

1. Sound is useful, dangerous, and readable.
2. The player causes many of the scares through their own choices.
3. Atmosphere supports gameplay rather than covering for missing gameplay.
4. The monster is understood through behavior before being clearly seen.
5. Scope stays finishable: one location, one systemic enemy, one signature mechanic.

## Kill criteria

Do not enter full production unless the vertical slice proves all five:

- Recording and replay are fun without finished art.
- Players understand why the threat heard them.
- A 20–25 minute slice produces tension without constant scripted jumps.
- The slice holds 60 FPS on the chosen minimum-spec test machine at an appropriate preset.
- At least four of five external testers want to continue after the slice.

If the mechanic fails, simplify it before adding content. Do not solve a weak loop with more hallways.
